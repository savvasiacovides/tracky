Road tyres that handle track days

For a car you drive every day, in all weather, and take on track a few times a season.

Our pick: Bridgestone Potenza Sport Evo. It won sport auto’s 2026 summer tyre test, came second in Tyre Reviews’ 2026 performance tyre test and had the shortest dry braking in ACE’s 2026 test.

It is also made in 17 inch sizes for smaller cars. The Pirelli P Zero R beat it in Tyre Reviews’ test, best in the dry and quickest in the wet, but Pirelli makes it for sports cars and supercars on 19 inch wheels and up. The Michelin Pilot Sport 4S won ACE’s 2026 test and is the long-standing favourite, with a 300 treadwear grade.

Road-legal track tyres

For regular track days in a car you drive to the circuit. More dry grip than any road tyre, with a shorter life and less grip in the rain.

Our pick: Pirelli P Zero Trofeo RS. It won Auto Bild’s 2026 track-day tyre test on a BMW M2 and Tyre Reviews’ test of the fastest track tyres, where it set the fastest dry lap.

The Michelin Pilot Sport Cup 2 R came second in that Tyre Reviews test and is fitted at the factory by Porsche, Ferrari and AMG. The Goodyear Eagle F1 SuperCar 3R is Goodyear’s most track-focused road-legal tyre, with a 100 treadwear grade, sold in Europe in 20 inch sizes. The Bridgestone Potenza Race stopped shortest in the wet in Tyre Reviews’ test, so it is the one for damp days.

200-treadwear tyres for class racing

For club series and time-attack classes that require a treadwear rating of 200 or more. Road legal, and close to a track tyre in the dry.

Our pick: Yokohama Advan A052. It is the all-rounder of the group, with the second-fastest wet lap in Tire Rack’s 2025 track test, and the only one of the four you can buy through Tracky.

In the dry the others were quicker in the 2025 and 2026 tests. The Vitour Tempesta P1 came second in Auto Bild’s 2026 track-day test and braked shortest in Tire Rack’s 2025 test. The Nankang Sportnex CR-S came third in Auto Bild’s test and was the fastest when worn in Grassroots Motorsports’ 2026 long-term test. The Bridgestone Potenza RE-71RS was best in four of five measures in Tire Rack’s 2025 test, wet included, but we could not find it on sale in Europe.

Semi-slicks for track cars

For a car used mainly on track, on dry days. 100-treadwear semi-slicks: the most grip of any road-legal tyre, and the least in the cold and wet.

Our pick: Nankang AR-1. It is a 100-treadwear semi-slick that is still E-marked for the road and on the MSUK List 1B for UK club racing, made in sizes from 13 to 20 inch.

The Toyo Proxes R888R is the other 100-treadwear classic, also from 13 inch. The Michelin Pilot Sport Cup 2 R is the road-legal choice for bigger, faster cars, made by Michelin for intense track use.
