Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026 · Version 1

Tracky is a lap timer and data logger for track days. This policy says what Tracky collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept and what you can do about it. It applies to the Tracky iPhone app and to Tracky's online services.

Who is responsible

Tracky is provided by Altivox Operations OÜ (registry code 17520583), Ahtri tn 12, 15551 Tallinn, Estonia, which is the controller of your personal data.
Privacy contact: privacy@trackyapp.eu

The short version

  • You can record and review your own laps without an account. That data stays on your iPhone.
  • An account (email and password) is needed only for online features: your profile, friends, the community, leaderboards and keeping your sessions on more than one device.
  • Your heart rate is never sold, never used for advertising and never given to partners.
  • We never sell data that identifies you. Partners get your details only if you choose to share them with that partner.
  • You can delete your account and its data from inside the app at any time, and ask us for a copy of your data.

What Tracky collects

Kept on your iPhone only (no account needed)

  • Recordings: GPS positions, speed, laps and sectors, engine data from an OBD reader (such as revs, speed, throttle and pedal position), G-forces from the phone's motion sensors, and heart rate from a strap or watch you connect.
  • Videos filmed with Tracky or added by you, and the cameras, GPS receivers and OBD readers you have used.
  • Your settings, cars and the tracks you have drawn.

None of this leaves your phone unless you send it (for example by exporting a file) or use one of the online features below.

When you ask to test Tracky

  • Tester request: the email address you give, and if you add them, your iPhone model, the devices you would use and where you drive.
  • We use it only to invite you to test Tracky through Apple's TestFlight and to email you about testing. It is kept until testing ends or you ask us to remove it (write to privacy@trackyapp.eu). Legal basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • If you are invited, Apple receives your email address to send the TestFlight invitation, under Apple's own privacy policy.

When you create an account

  • Account: your email address and a password (stored only as a secure hash), an account identifier, and your date of birth (to confirm you are 16 or over; kept apart, never shown).
  • Profile: the driver name you choose and, if you add them, your car and its details, and a profile photo.
  • Synced sessions: if you switch on sync, the recordings listed above, so they appear on your other devices. Videos are not uploaded.

When you use online features

  • Leaderboards: your best lap at each track, with your driver name, car, the track, the direction driven and the date.
  • Community: posts, photos and text you publish, the friends you add, ratings, reports and blocks.
  • Track weather: the location of a track is sent to Apple Weather to fetch its forecast. Your own location is not sent.

Automatically

  • Usage and diagnostics: which features are used, crashes and performance, the app version and the iPhone model. These are not linked to your name.

Tracky does not collect your contacts, does not read which other apps you have and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites unless you allow it (see Advertising).

Anonymous statistics

Tracky may combine data from many drivers into statistics, for example how busy a circuit is, typical lap times by type of car, or which equipment drivers use, and may share or sell these statistics to circuits, organisers, parts and tyre makers and other businesses in motorsport. Statistics are only made from groups large enough that no single driver can be picked out, and never include names, email addresses, heart rate or individual GPS traces. You can switch this off in Settings › Privacy.

Advertising

Tracky may show advertising or sponsored content. By default it is chosen by context (for example the track you are looking at), not by a profile of you. Personalised advertising, or anything that follows you across other companies' apps and websites, happens only if you agree, both in Tracky and in Apple's "Allow Tracking" prompt. Heart rate is never used for advertising.

Partner offers

Partners such as track-day organisers or circuits may make offers through Tracky. Your details are given to a partner only if you choose to share them with that partner, and the app shows exactly what will be shared before you do. You can stop sharing with a partner at any time; what they already received is then covered by their own privacy policy.

Premium subscription

Subscriptions are bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment; Tracky never receives your card details, only whether your subscription is active.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase hosts accounts, profiles and synced data in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Apple (iCloud and CloudKit for community features and leaderboards, Apple Weather, the App Store).
  • Advertising and analytics providers, when introduced, will be listed here before they are used.

Where a provider may access data from outside the European Economic Area, the transfer is covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

What you publish to leaderboards and the community is visible to other Tracky users.

How long it is kept

  • Account, profile and synced sessions: while you have an account. When you delete your account they are deleted within 30 days, and from backups within 90 days.
  • Community posts: until you delete them or your account.
  • Reports about abuse: up to 12 months.
  • Diagnostics: up to 12 months.
  • Anonymous statistics are not personal data and may be kept.

Your rights and choices

  • In the app: Settings › Account lets you switch sync, heart rate and anonymous statistics on or off, and delete your account. To get a copy of your data or change your email, write to us at the address below.
  • Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, object to or restrict its use, and withdraw consent at any time, without affecting what was done before. Write to privacy@trackyapp.eu. We answer within one month.
  • You can complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (aki.ee) or the authority where you live.

Children

Tracky is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. When an account is created we ask for the date of birth and make no account for anyone under 16. The date of birth is kept apart from the profile, is never shown to other drivers, and is used only to confirm age. If you believe a child under 16 has an account, write to privacy@trackyapp.eu and we will delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, passwords are stored only as hashes, and access to each person's data is limited to that person by the database's own access rules.

Changes

When this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date, and the app tells you about any change to how your data is used before it applies. Anything that needs your consent is asked for again.
