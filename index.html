Press START. The laps time themselves.

Tracky is a lap timer for track days on iPhone. It finds the line, times every lap and sector, and shows you afterwards where the time went.

Speed, revs, throttle, pedal and G along the lap, one lap laid over another. Pinch in on a single corner.

Drive it, then watch it back.

While you drive

Lap clock and gap to your best, big enough to read at a glance. Swipe between screens you set up yourself, or see them on your car's screen with CarPlay.

Lap times can be spoken as you cross the line.

Video with your figures

Film with the iPhone or a GoPro. Export any lap with your speed, revs, gap and track map drawn on it, in the instrument look you choose.

Videos from other cameras line up by themselves from the beep Tracky plays at START.

Works with the gear you have.

The iPhone alone is enough to start. Add a GPS receiver for ten or more positions a second, and an OBD reader for the car's own data.

GPS receivers10 to 25 positions a second
RaceBox Mini, Mini S and Micro. Qstarz BL-1000GT, BL-1000ST, BL-818GT and LT-8000GT. Dragy. Racelogic VBOX Sport. Dual XGPS150 and XGPS160. Bad Elf. Columbus, PGEAR and other receivers that send NMEA or u-blox data.
Car datathrough an OBD reader
OBDLink MX+, Carista and most Bluetooth readers that speak ELM327. Revs, speed, throttle, pedal, load and temperatures, as often as the car answers.
Cameras
GoPro HERO9 and later, MAX2 and MISSION 1 start and stop with your session. The iPhone's own camera films too.
Heart rate
Garmin, Polar, Wahoo and other straps and watches that broadcast heart rate.
Your files
Sessions stay on your iPhone. Export CSV, VBO, GPX or a RaceChrono file whenever you want another tool, and import your RaceChrono sessions.
