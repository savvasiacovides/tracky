Press START. Drive.

No track database to download and no lines to set. Tracky finds the start line at a known circuit or learns it on your first lap.

Mount and press START

Use the iPhone's own GPS, or connect a GPS receiver and an OBD reader for more detail.

Drive your session

Big, glanceable figures: lap clock, gap to best, last and best lap. Lap times can be spoken as you cross the line.

See where the time went

Stop, and the session is saved with every lap, sector and corner, ready to compare.

Built for finding time

Everything a track-day driver needs, without the clutter.

Automatic lap timing

Laps and sectors timed from the line you cross, with a live gap to your best lap and a predicted lap time.

Coach

Every lap laid over your best, corner by corner: where you braked, the slowest point, the exit speed, and the places with the most time to gain.

Graphs you can read

Speed, revs, throttle, G-forces and more, along the lap. Pinch to zoom into a single corner and compare with another lap or session.

Video with your data

Film with the iPhone or add a video from another camera, then export it with your speed, revs, lap time and map drawn over it.

Engine data

Revs, speed, throttle and pedal position read from your car through an OBD reader, as often as the car allows.

Your data, your files

Sessions stay on your iPhone. Export to CSV, VBO, GPX or RaceChrono when you want to use another tool.

Works with the gear you have

The iPhone is enough to start. Add a receiver for ten or more positions a second, and an OBD reader for the car's own data.

GPS receivers

  • RaceBox Mini, Mini S and Micro
  • Qstarz BL-1000GT, BL-1000ST, BL-818GT, LT-8000GT
  • Dragy
  • Racelogic VBOX Sport
  • Dual XGPS150 and XGPS160, Bad Elf
  • Receivers that send NMEA or u-blox data over Bluetooth

OBD readers and more

  • OBDLink MX+ and Carista
  • Most Bluetooth LE readers that speak ELM327
  • Heart-rate straps and watches that broadcast heart rate
  • GoPro cameras with Open GoPro over Bluetooth
