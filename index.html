Works with the gear you have.

  • GPS receivers

    10 to 25 positions a second, for laps timed to the hundredth.

    RaceBox, Qstarz, Dragy, VBOX Sport, Dual XGPS, Bad Elf, NMEA and u-blox receivers

  • OBD readers

    The car's own revs, throttle, pedal and temperatures.

    OBDLink MX+, Carista and most Bluetooth ELM327 readers

  • Action cameras

    Start and stop with your session, lined up to the frame.

    GoPro HERO9 and later, MAX2, MISSION 1

  • Heart rate

    Beat by beat, laid over every lap.

    Garmin, Polar, Wahoo and any strap or watch that broadcasts

  • Just your iPhone

    Its own GPS, motion sensors and camera are enough to start.

    iPhone on iOS 26

  • CarPlay

    The gap to best, large, on the car's display. With Tracky Pro.
