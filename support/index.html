Lap timing

Do I need to set up the track first?

No. At a circuit Tracky knows, the start line is found for you. Anywhere else, Tracky places the line on your first lap. You can also draw your own start, finish and sector lines under You › Tracks.

Why does my lap time look a little off with the iPhone's GPS?

The iPhone's own GPS gives one position a second, so a lap can be out by a fraction of a second. A GPS receiver that sends ten or more positions a second makes timing much more precise.

Can Tracky tell me my lap times while I drive?

Yes. Switch on spoken lap times in Settings. They are spoken while Tracky is on screen.

Devices

My GPS receiver or OBD reader doesn't appear

Make sure it is switched on, charged and not connected to another app or phone. Open Drive › Devices › Add. Readers such as the OBDLink MX+ and receivers such as the VBOX Sport pair through the iPhone's Bluetooth settings first. The add screen also lists the reasons a device may be missing.

Which OBD values can Tracky read?

Whatever your car offers through the standard OBD interface, usually revs, speed, throttle, accelerator pedal, engine load and temperatures. Brake pressure is not part of the standard and is not available.

How do I connect a GoPro?

Put the camera in pairing mode the first time, then add it in Devices. Tracky starts and stops filming with START and Stop and lines the video up with your data.

Video

The video is the wrong way round

The video takes the shape of the phone when you press START: upright gives an upright video, on its side a wide one. Turn the phone the way you want before pressing START.

How do I export a video with my data on it?

Open the session, go to Video and choose Export with data. You can export one lap or the whole session.

Your account and data

Do I need an account?

No. Recording and reviewing your own laps works without one. An account is only needed for online features such as leaderboards and the community.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings › Account and choose Delete account. Your account and its data are deleted. See the Privacy Policy for details, or write to privacy@trackyapp.eu.
